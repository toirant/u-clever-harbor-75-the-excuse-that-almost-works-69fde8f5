## The entry

> Sorry, my router died overnight and the engineer can't come till Thursday, I can't log in from home either

19 words, one line, 106 characters.

## Approach

A tired manager has heard every excuse twice, so the line wins by being
boring, specific, and hard to argue with: a dead home router, and an
engineer who cannot come until Thursday. It pre-empts the obvious
work-from-home fallback ("I can't log in from home either") and it never
mentions the game — which is the whole point. No dead grandmother, no flat
tyre, no sudden fever.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, checking it against the
competition gate first: one line of plain text, at most 20 words, 1 to 140
characters.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
